Program Overview

Inspire (Strength) Research Circle

Using biomechanics and psychology as twin axes, we integrate body awareness with emotional literacy to build a holistic teacher development platform.

Core Philosophy

This community is built around "strength-based inspiration," combining physical training with Social-Emotional Learning (SEL). We help teachers — amid the demands of their professional lives — establish personal mind-body balance through regular physical training and mindfulness practice, then carry that ethos into the classroom to enhance student learning outcomes.

Three Key Deliverables

  • 8 teacher community workshops (7 internal facilitators, 1 external expert)
  • 6 digital teaching videos for online co-learning and independent practice
  • 1 complete "SEL Digital Practice Portfolio & Emotion Feedback System"
SEL Strength Training Mindful Stretching Digital Literacy Stress Management AI Collaboration

Four Implementation Dimensions

  • (1) SEL Body-Mind Awareness & Regulation
    Using equipment training and breath guidance, we cultivate present-moment focus during exercise to achieve mind-body balance and stress management.
  • (2) Digital Video Content Production
    Six teaching videos are produced to break spatial limits, enabling online co-learning and independent practice anytime.
  • (3) AI-Integrated Teaching Design & Auto-Feedback System
    Developing an "SEL Digital Practice Portfolio & Emotion Feedback System" as a model for AI-integrated assessment. GAS auto-computes emotional data and delivers personalized HTML reports via Gmail.
  • (4) Digital Tools & Learning Assessment for Iterative Improvement
    Digital tools manage learning portfolios, collect per-session feedback, and auto-aggregate data — eliminating manual work and accelerating impact analysis.
  • (5) Extending Teaching Impact
    Enhancing teachers' digital literacy and applying body-mind awareness and SEL principles in the classroom to elevate student learning outcomes.
Workshop Schedule

8-Session Development Series

Each session is a conversation between body and mind — from building foundations to deep integration, growing together step by step.

01

Body Assessment & Functional Movement Activation

Grounded in biomechanics, participants assess their current physical condition, establish correct movement patterns and training safety concepts — laying the groundwork for the series.

Internal
02

Core Stability & Spinal Protection Training

Targeting teachers who stand and sit for long periods, this session strengthens the core muscle group to prevent lower back pain and build a solid structural support system.

Internal
03

Mindful Body Scan & Intro to Hatha Yoga

Combining mindfulness meditation with Hatha Yoga, participants deepen body awareness through sustained postures and breath regulation, releasing chronic tension.

Internal
04

Resistance Training & Equipment Practice

Introduces safe operation of diverse training equipment with progressive resistance design to improve overall muscular endurance and boost metabolism.

Internal
05

Flow Yoga & Dynamic Stress Release

Using flowing movement sequences as the medium, rhythmic breathing activates the full fascial system and releases accumulated stress and emotional tension.

Internal
06

SEL Emotional Regulation & Mind-Body Integration

The SEL framework is applied to analyze the psychological benefits of physical practice, designing classroom-transferable emotional regulation activities to strengthen SEL capacity for teachers and students alike.

Internal
07

Digital Portfolio Review & Peer Collaboration

Individual practice data and emotional portfolios are reviewed through peer sharing and reflective dialogue, building community consensus and co-planning classroom application.

Internal
08

Expert Lecture: Physical Release & Autonomic Nervous System Regulation

An external expert shares in-depth physical release techniques and autonomic nervous system regulation strategies, providing cross-disciplinary professional insight and practical tools.
